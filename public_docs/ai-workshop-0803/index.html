8 月 3 日場次的獨立診斷

本場次是第一階段課程隨 AI 工具與使用情境持續調整後的再優化版本。課程以資料整理、教案或講義草稿及實際工作流程為主軸，回應了 68.8% 教師希望以 AI Agent 整合資料產出教學素材的需求；後測亦有 57.9% 教師認為資料導向的 AI Agent 基礎模組最有幫助。

16 組匿名配對教師的五項自我效能總平均，從 2.94 分提升至 4.04 分，平均增加 1.10 分；75.0% 呈正向改變。教學實務幫助平均為 4.68/5，94.7% 給予 4 或 5 分，NPS 為 63。

判讀：近期的優化不只是更新工具內容，而是讓教師更能把 AI Agent 連結到自己的教學與工作流程。

綜合結論

本系列工作坊已由早期試行，逐步發展成能穩定獲得正向回饋的課程模式。講師隨 AI 工具與使用情境更新內容，並以可操作的流程與案例呈現，能有效回應教師需求。建議持續辦理，並將學術誠信、資料隱私、跨領域案例與課後任務納入下一階段，以讓短期信心轉化為可持續的實務應用。

解讀界限

三場皆為單場次、無對照組的自陳問卷，且參與者背景與樣本數不同。結果支持課程持續優化的趨勢性證據，但不能直接推論長期教學行為或學生學習成效。

August 3 standalone diagnosis

This refined first-stage workshop connected AI Agent concepts to data organization, draft teaching materials, and real workflows. It aligned with the leading pre-session need: 68.8% wanted help turning data into teaching materials, while 57.9% selected the data-based AI Agent foundations module as most helpful.

Across 16 anonymous matched respondents, average self-efficacy rose from 2.94 to 4.04, a gain of 1.10 points. Practical-helpfulness averaged 4.68 out of 5, 94.7% rated it 4 or 5, and NPS was 63.

Comparison across three sessions

The July 2 pilot showed a modest gain and identified needs for pacing, onboarding, and disciplinary relevance. Following refinement, both July 9 and August 3 showed stronger self-efficacy gains, high practical-helpfulness ratings, and positive recommendation intent. The August 3 result therefore supports repeatable improvement rather than an isolated positive response.

Overall conclusion

The series has progressed from an early pilot to a refined workshop format with consistently strong later-session feedback. Continue the program while strengthening academic-integrity guidance, privacy checks, disciplinary examples, and post-workshop activities to convert confidence into sustained practice.

Interpretation limits

These are single-session, self-report surveys without a control group. Participant composition and sample sizes differ across sessions, so the evidence supports an improvement trend rather than a causal or long-term outcome claim.
